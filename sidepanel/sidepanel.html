Welcome to StudyLens

StudyLens turns a webpage or your own study files into a summary, key points, and a full reviewer you can study from.

  • StudyLens reads a webpage only when you click "Scan Current Page."
  • Uploaded files are read locally in your browser to extract text - see Settings for exactly what's sent to your AI provider.
  • It never needs your camera, microphone, or location.
  • AI features send the extracted text to your chosen AI service for processing.
